# Research

## Integrated Quantum Photonics Lab @ MIT
My research group focuses on developing scalable photonic quantum technologies that overcome current limitations in qubit coherence, scalability, and manufacturability. We combine:
- Nanofabrication of ultra-high-Q photonic devices
- Solid-state quantum emitter engineering
- Quantum information theory for error correction
- System-level integration for real-world deployment

### Current Projects
1. **1000-Qubit Photonic Quantum Processor** (NSQLI Grant, 2024–2028)
   - Developing programmable silicon photonic qubit array with >99.5% gate fidelity
   - Integrating on-chip quantum memory for error correction

2. **Room-Temperature Quantum Communication Network** (ARO Grant, 2023–2026)
   - Field-testing integrated QKD transmitters over fiber and free-space links
   - Developing quantum repeaters for continental-scale networks

3. **Quantum Photonic Materials** (NSF CAREER, 2024–2029)
   - Engineering color centers in silicon carbide for telecom-wavelength quantum emitters
   - Heterogeneous integration with silicon photonics platforms
